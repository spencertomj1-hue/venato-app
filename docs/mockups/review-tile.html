Review tile: three directions

The tile today is seven little bars and a header that mostly reads "None confirmed yet". Each set below fixes that with the numbers review.summary() already works out, so nothing new needs logging. Every set covers Small and Wide (the sizes the tile has now) plus Large, which it would gain.

Example week: Mon 16 – Sun 22 Nov, it's Sunday afternoon. 4 of 6 finished days confirmed, 74% of plan followed (66% last week), Study 6 of 10 h, 14 of 19 to-dos, review opens at 7pm.
