What changes in the code

  • Header status (tile_status.review): Small shows the verdict word from review.headline(); Wide and Large show hours and to-dos, since the sentence is in the tile.
  • Wide fix: its two halves were stacking on top of each other instead of sitting side by side, so the bottom ran off the tile. They now sit side by side, hours and to-dos moved into the header, and the bars are shorter. Checked: nothing in any tile here crosses its bottom edge on a 180px row.
  • Unconfirmed days are hatched, not grey, with a count to confirm.
  • Large is new: it gets added to the tile's sizes.
